Design Your Custom Lab Diamond Ring

Crafted with precision, tailored to your story. Every carat, setting, and detail is fully customizable.

At Sergio Christoph, we believe true luxury is personal. Our bespoke process allows you to engineer your dream ring step-by-step, using sustainably created lab diamonds that possess the exact brilliance, clarity, and durability of mined diamonds.

Step 01

Select Your Diamond Shape

The foundation of your piece begins with the cut of your lab diamond. Select from a wide variety of classic and fancy silhouettes.

Brilliant & Classic Cuts Round, Oval, Cushion, Elongated Cushion
Step Cuts & Architectural Lines Emerald, Asscher, Baguette, Radiant
Distinctive & Statement Shapes Princess, Heart, Marquise, Pear
Step 02

Choose Your Setting

The setting anchors your diamond and defines how light enters the stone.

Minimalist & Secure Basket, Peg Head
Sleek & Contemporary Bezel, Half Bezel
Intricate & Decorative Channel, Decorative, Bright Cut
Step 03

Select Your Pavé Details

Add extra sparkle along the band with meticulous accent diamond placement.

Modern & Subtle Mini, Petite, French
Textured & Sculptural U-Cut, Scalloped, Bright Cut
Step 04

Choose Your Prongs

Prongs provide structural integrity while framing your diamond's shape.

Classic Frames Four Prong, Six Prong, Double Prong
Delicate & Modern Touches Button Prong, Compass Four Prong, Baby Claw Prong
Step 05

Define Your Overall Style

Unify your selections into a cohesive master architecture.

Timeless Elegance Solitaire, Halo
Subtle Luxury Hidden Halo, Bridge
Continuous Brilliance Pavé

Custom Carat & Metal Selection

Every model can be adapted to your desired carat size and crafted in your choice of precious metal colors.

World-Class Certification

Your custom diamond comes fully authenticated with official certification from international gemological institutes such as GIA or IGI, ensuring complete clarity and transparency.
