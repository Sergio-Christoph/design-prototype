The Lab

Grown by light, in ninety days

The same brilliance, clarity and durability as a mined diamond — engineered, not extracted.

01 — Seed

A sliver of pure carbon

Every stone begins as a Type IIa diamond seed, placed in the growth chamber.

02 — Growth

Atom by atom, by light

In a plasma of hydrogen and methane, carbon settles onto the seed. Ninety days, one crystal.

03 — Cut & polish

Shaped to Excellent

Master cutters facet the rough to Excellent proportions, releasing its fire.

04 — Certification

Graded, stone by stone

GIA and IGI grade every diamond independently — cut, color, clarity, carat.
